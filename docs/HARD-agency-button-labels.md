# HARD — Agency button labels show both languages

**Effective:** 2026-09-29 ~07:32 CT
**Owner:** Chris Olsen (via Helios)
**Scope:** Live Rocket Tracker agency and vehicle labels. No exceptions.

## Absolute rule

1. Every foreign-language label must also show English alongside it. Chris cannot read Russian or other languages.
2. For any other country's agency or vehicle, the label must display in that country's language. No exceptions.

Both lines are required. English alone is not enough for a non-English agency. The foreign language alone is not enough, because Chris has to be able to read it.

## Examples

- Chinese plates already follow this. English on top, Chinese with it.
- Roscosmos: Russian and English.
- ESA: French and English.
- Any future agency: that country's language and English.

US agencies stay English. This rule does not invent a second language for them.

## Not this

- Do not publish or submit to Play.
- Do not drop the English line to make a plate "native only."
- Do not leave a non-English agency in English only.
