## Full description
Live Rocket Tracker is a mission-control live wallpaper for people who actually watch launches.

It is not a news app. It is a HUD. Put it on a home-screen page, swipe to it, and the stack is in front of you.

FREE
• Live countdown to the next launch
• Eight HUD plates: CMD, CDT, TEL, STS, PAD, VID, MSK, AUTO
• Real vehicle outlines from published hardware, not cartoon rockets
• PAD drawing and coordinates. Tap coords to open the pad in Google Earth
• AUTO browses the next upcoming flight

Enough to know if this is for you. Nobody pays to find out the HUD is a command center.

PRO UNLOCKS
• Historic launches. Go back and watch flights you missed.
• YouTube picture-in-picture next to the HUD, with your YouTube login.
• The tracker stays about 90 seconds ahead of the webcast on purpose. Live data first. Then the video, so you see what actually happened.
• LCK pins the flight you are watching so it does not jump.
• Rumble and launch cues.
• Command Center, the full console.

HOW TO USE IT
1. Set Live Rocket Tracker as your live wallpaper.
2. In settings, pick which home-screen page is the HUD. The eight plates only draw on that page. Swipe away and they disappear. That is intended.
3. AUTO browses the next launch. LCK (Pro) pins the one you are watching.
4. VID (Pro) is the webcast in picture-in-picture. Keep YouTube signed in.
5. The HUD leading the TV delay is intended. If something looks wrong on the tracker, look at the video.
6. PAD coordinates are a link.

This is a niche tool. If you want a pretty countdown widget, this is the wrong app. If you want to sit on the pad with the stack, this is it.

Not affiliated with SpaceX, NASA, or any launch provider. Vehicle drawings follow published public hardware.
