DFW trucking network — daily power (verified vs EST)
Updated 2026-08-20. Christopher Olsen. Do not invent beyond what is labeled EST.

UNITS
- Instantaneous power: GW (what the array can put out in the sun)
- Daily energy: GWh/day (what actually matters for a dock that runs today)
- 1 TW = 1,000 GW. 1 TWh/day = 1,000 GWh/day.
- A truck burns energy per mile (kWh/mi), not per year.

DAILY ROOF ENERGY — EST, assumptions stated
Usable-roof fraction and W/SF are NOT published for DFW. Bands:

  conservative: 40% of Partners W/D footprint * 10 W/SF = 3.55 GW DC → 14.5 GWh/day
  mid:         55% * 12 W/SF                         = 5.86 GW DC → 23.9 GWh/day
  aggressive:  65% * 15 W/SF                         = 8.66 GW DC → 35.3 GWh/day
  mid logistics (CoStar 937M SF, 55% * 12 W):          6.18 GW DC → 25.2 GWh/day

Daily energy = nameplate kW * 4.08 kWh/kW-day.

NOT TERAWATTS. Mid case is about 24 GWh per day.
1 TW running 24 hours would be 24,000 GWh/day. Off by about 1,000×.
The mix-up is power (GW) vs energy (GWh/day) vs yearly TWh.

TRAILER SOLAR — EST, statewide plates only
375 SF * 12 W/SF = 4.5 kW per trailer EST.
197,757 Texas token trailers * 4.5 kW = 0.89 GW IF every plate is a 53-ft box in full sun.
Daily: 3.6 GWh/day. That is ALL TEXAS, not DFW, and moving/parked/shaded is not published.

TRUCK USE PER MILE — his number
2.0 kWh/mi.
Mid roofs 23.9 GWh/day ÷ 2.0 kWh/mi = 12.0 million truck-miles per day of energy.
Conservative 7.2 million. Aggressive 17.7 million.

What that is NOT: a published DFW truck-mile count. It is how many miles the roofs can feed
at his 2.0 kWh/mi. Until NCTCOG/FAF truck VMT is pulled, do not claim the fleet uses X GWh/day.

Per truck-day (miles/day is NOT published; leave as a slider for the Unity sim):
  200 mi/day local  * 2.0 = 400 kWh/truck-day. Mid roofs ≈ 60,000 truck-days.
  400 mi/day regional * 2.0 = 800 kWh/truck-day. Mid roofs ≈ 30,000 truck-days.
  600 mi/day long    * 2.0 = 1,200 kWh/truck-day. Mid roofs ≈ 20,000 truck-days.

THE POINT
Warehouse roofs in DFW are a several-gigawatt plant and a couple-dozen GWh per day.
That is nuclear-plant scale, no new utility feeder. It is not a terawatt.
The strip + trailer + industrial bank is how that daily energy actually reaches a truck
without melting the building service drop.
