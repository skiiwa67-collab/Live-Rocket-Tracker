================================================================
HYBRID / SECOND-AXLE ANALOGS (published)
================================================================
1) Eaton parallel hybrid Class 8, Coca-Cola, NREL 13-month field
   https://www.nrel.gov/docs/fy12osti/53502.pdf
   In service: hybrid group 5.63 mpg, +13.7% vs the diesel pair.
   Chassis dyno: +30.3% WVU City, +22% CILCC.
   Highway-ish CARB HHDDT: statistically no difference.
   This is the honest "in a real fleet" number: teens of percent,
   and the win is the city cycle.

2) UC Davis Class 8 hybrid simulation (various fuels)
   Diesel hybrid vs diesel: +24% day-drive, +10% short-haul,
   +3–6% long-haul.
   Same story. Duty cycle is the whole game.

3) SAE 02-19-04-0025 (2026) P2 / P2-P3 / P4 Class 8
   P2 blended charge-depleting+sustaining: +12–14% real-world cycles.
   Charge-sustaining only: +4–6%.
   Closest "second electric path on a diesel truck" paper.

4) Range Energy RA-01 — electric DRIVE AXLE ON THE TRAILER
   MVTS on-road, 25.5 mi urban/highway loop, ~59,000 lb, 60 mph:
   +36.3% (3.82 mpg) system ON vs OFF.
   Stop/go slice +36.9%. Steady slice +35.3%.
   Battery was charged before the test. That is not free diesel.
   Long-haul they have said 10–20% in interviews.
   Architecture is a powered trailer, not his tractor pod.
   Useful as "a second driven axle helps a lot." Not his mpg.

5) Revoy electric DOLLY claiming 20–35 mpg
   Vendor claim, 575 kWh swappable dolly, 22,000 lb.
   Different machine. Do not use as the Power Pod number.

================================================================
WHAT THAT MEANS ON A DAY LIKE HIS (EST, labeled)
================================================================
Pick 230 miles (middle of 180–280). Diesel EST 6.0 mpg:
  230 / 6.0 = 38.3 gallons.

If the pod behaves like the Coca-Cola in-service hybrid (+13.7%):
  6.8 mpg → 33.7 gal. Save ~4.6 gal / day.

If it behaves like the city-cycle hybrids (+25% EST):
  7.5 mpg → 30.7 gal. Save ~7.6 gal / day.

If it behaves like highway-only (+5%):
  6.3 mpg → 36.5 gal. Save ~1.8 gal / day. Almost nothing.

His DFW day is the city-cycle case. That is why the write-off
should hit P&D / shuttle / blind-haul first, not linehaul.
(Linehaul was a dictation mixup. His work is not that.)

We have NOT measured a diesel+pod Kenworth. When we have a
prototype, we datalog HIS 180–280 and replace this EST band.

================================================================
FIRST PRINCIPLES
================================================================
Fuel is burned to make heat and motion. In P&D you throw the
motion away at every light and every dock. Regen puts it back
in the pack. The second axle is a motor-generator. Highway
cruise has almost nothing to regen, so the diesel still does
almost all the work. Tesla Semi at Pepsi / Frito-Lay is the
other end: no diesel, ~2 kWh/mi. Different product.

Best part is no part: do not hybrid the linehaul fleet first.
Hybrid the trucks that stop.
