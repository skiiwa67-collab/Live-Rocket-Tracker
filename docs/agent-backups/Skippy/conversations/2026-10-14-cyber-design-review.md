# Red-team: DESIGN-cyber-attack.md (Skippy, 2026-10-14)
Reviewed against Skippy's "Cyber attack path" section in Team/bugout-decision-logic-PROPOSAL.md and Chris's answers: 2 down = watch, 3+ = warning, water+power or hospital/911 = warning at 2, unknown = show both (all adjustable). Ranked; § = DESIGN-cyber-attack.md.

1. **ABUSE: panic seeding.** §5 counts distinct *services*, so an adversary who seeds 3 services in 3 cells gets a "cascading failures" warning (§11 drill is exactly that pattern). §2 delegates sybil checks to tornado rules, which have no spread rule. Fix: per service, require reports from ≥ N independent cells spread over ≥ T minutes, plus the device-signal cross-check (phone's own no-service, no-data) from Skippy §2–3. All are Params; below the bar it shows "reported, not confirmed", never hidden.
2. **AMBIGUOUS → Chris first: does "warning at 2" fire on crowd reports alone?** §5 plus §9 let crowd-only water+power reach a warning while officials are unreachable. That's a high-panic combination and an easy one to seed. Ask Chris whether crowd-only water+power should start at watch and upgrade only with a device signal, a utility notice, or extra spread.
3. **FAILURE: correlated non-cyber outages.** One substation drops power, cell, payments and traffic lights. §5's dependency chain only handles parent→child timing. A "power-first, children within battery-backup hours" pattern should collapse to ONE root event *regardless of order*. Fix: a root-cause clustering step, plus water pumps as a power child (§5 lists it, so make its lag a Param).
4. **ABUSE: false all-clear / suppression.** No section covers how a service is marked restored. An adversary can flood "working again" reports. Fix: restoration needs the same corroboration as "down" plus time-decay (§4 half-life). The alert ends only via an official all-clear or the user's own dismiss (OQ #17), and comes back on new evidence.
5. **FAILURE: carrier outage hides reports.** When cell and data are down, the people who can report are only those on mesh or another carrier, so counts fall exactly as the event worsens. Fix: treat "no reports" from a cell as UNKNOWN, not OK, when that cell's last-heard time is old. Show "few reports: comms down here".
6. **ABUSE: tracking people.** §3 has a 1 km cell and k-anonymity 3. But per-report timestamps plus a rare service combination still fingerprint a home, and repeat reports link one device across cells. Fix: per-incident rotating keys (Skippy §8), time bucketed to a Param (start 10 min), k-anonymity applied per (cell, service, time-bucket), and raw retention 24 h then aggregate only.
7. **FAILURE: mesh flooding.** §9 relays every signed delta. A sybil or burst can saturate the mesh and starve SOS. Fix: aggregate before relaying (per cell/service counts, not raw reports), per-origin rate limits (Param), and a relay priority below SOS and location.
8. **ABSOLUTES / hardcoded.** §3 calls privacy a "hard design rule". It's a fine invariant, but it should be stated as a non-number rule. §2 "default 1 km" uses metric; show it as ~0.6 mi in US units. §8 "Chris signs off" on art conflicts with "each bot okays its own art" except for LRT, so ask Chris. §6 SWPC "G4-G5" is a fixed scale band and should be a Param. §1 "one full day" is fine as an estimate.
9. **GAP: GPS spoofing/jamming is not a service tile.** It's in Skippy's list but missing from §2's table, and cyber attacks on GNSS directly hurt routing. Fix: add a GPS tile plus auto-detection (fix jumps, cell/Wi-Fi mismatch). A spoofed GPS must never be used as a report's own location.
10. **IDEA: "your own readiness" card.** When the stack is ≥ watch, show the user's own checklist (water stored, cash, fuel, meds, charged phones), adjusted to their profile (pets, kids, a medical device on power). It's already precaution-level-safe (§6 line 54). Wording for cash, fuel and water is UNSOURCED.
11. **IDEA: device-side passive signals with consent.** These count with a lower weight than people (Skippy §1): payment-app failures, the carrier's "no service", time-sync anomalies, 911 call failure. They give independent evidence that's harder to sybil than taps.
12. **GAP: §7 reporting guidance points to org-facing CISA text, and IC3 is unfetched.** Fix: mark it UNSOURCED for individuals until fetched. Don't show the phone number as a personal step.
