PAD — LANDING-GEAR ENERGY FACE
SPEC. Invented hardware. Does not exist in the field.
Christopher Olsen. Locked 2026-08-20. Gen 1 activation = MECHANICAL.

================================================================
WHAT IT IS
================================================================
A rubber strip laid parallel to the dock doors —
a run along the door line. Layout only.
Recessed in the slab. Flush with the concrete.
Wear face even with the yard so forklifts roll over it.
Not a raised bar.

Housing plus a consumable wear face.
Landing-gear 10 × 10 cushion feet land ON TOP of the
flush face. The face compresses and becomes active.
HV conduit runs UP INSIDE the landing-gear legs.
Not a cassette under the foot. The shoe sits on the strip.
Not poured-in copper. Not a charger bolted to a dock wall.
Doors do not switch the pad. No doors on the mechanism.

Landing gear walks. Forklifts chew the yard. The face dies.
You snap the cassette out. You snap a new one in.

Two shoes on one strip. Same cassette family for a 53-ft van
and a 28-ft pup.

================================================================
THE PATH
================================================================
BANK
  → PAD FACE (flush rubber cassette in the slab)
  → GEAR SHOE ON TOP OF THE FLUSH FACE
  → HV CONDUIT UP INSIDE THE LANDING-GEAR LEG
  → TRAILER JUNCTION
  → SNAP-IN MODULES
  → POWER POD / BEV PACK
  and back to the building.

Building bank feeds a pad bus under the slab.
Multiple tie-ins. Not one feeder.

LAYOUT (not the switch):
The rubber strip runs parallel to the dock doors.
A run along the door line. Doors do not switch the pad.
No doors on the mechanism drawing.

MECHANISM:
Shoe sits on top of the flush face. Weight compresses the
face. That segment conducts. Conduit runs up inside
the legs. The pad is recessed — even with the yard.

Trailer-skin PV can feed the modules without the pad.
The pad also lets the modules backfeed the bank.

================================================================
WEIGHT IS THE SWITCH
================================================================
Only the loaded segment conducts.
Idle pads stay dark.

================================================================
GEN 1 ACTIVATION — LOCKED MECHANICAL
================================================================
Close is a mechanical plunger / rocker / contact under gear weight.

The pressure-conductive material does not exist.
Do not wait for it. Do not draw it as the hero.

Later families, one note only, not Gen 1:
  - pressure-conductive material
  - hybrid (material plus mechanical)
